Printable flashcards for the 1,500 most commonly used English words and their translations into 15 languages. The PDFs are in **[`All_15_languages_-_1500_word_flashcards.zip`](All_15_languages_-_1500_word_flashcards.zip)**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Please read the [limitations](#limitations) before you study with these cards.** The translations have not been checked by native speakers, and they leave out tense and gender.

## What's on each card

Every PDF uses the **same 1,500 English words in the same order**. The cards are numbered 1–1500, so card #742 is the same word in every language.

- **Left half:** the word written in the target language and its own script.
- **Right half:** the pronunciation guide in italics, where there is one, with the **English word in bold** below it.
- **Homonyms are labelled.** Where an English word has more than one meaning, the card says which one it means. Some examples are *bear (animal)*, *bank (money)*, *date (calendar)*, *change (noun)* and *you (singular)*. This shows you which meaning the translation is for.
- **Notes on grammar.** Some English words have no direct equivalent in a language. For example, Japanese, Russian and Turkish have no word for "the". Those cards show a dash (—) and an explanatory note, such as *(no article in Japanese)*.

Each PDF has 125 pages, US Letter size (8.5 × 11 in), with 12 cards per page. The language name, a reminder of how to cut and fold, and the page number are printed in the page header.

## How to make the flashcards

1. **Print one-sided.** Turn off double-sided/duplex printing. Print at 100% / "Actual size" so the cards come out evenly sized.
2. **Cut along the solid lines.** This separates each page into 12 strips.
3. **Fold each strip along the dashed line** down the middle. The target-language word ends up on one face and the English word on the other.

Cardstock or heavier paper makes the cards more durable. A dab of glue or a glue stick inside the fold keeps them flat.

You don't need to print every page. Each page holds 12 consecutive words from the list, so you can print and study the cards in batches.

## Limitations

Please keep these in mind:

- **No tense or gender.** Each word is given in a single base form. Verb tenses and conjugations aren't shown. In languages with grammatical gender, or with separate masculine and feminine forms of a word, the masculine form has generally been used where there is a choice. The feminine form and other variants are usually missing.
- **Verbs always include "to".** English verbs are written in the infinitive, e.g. *to be*, *to go*, *to eat*. The translation is the dictionary form of the verb, not a conjugated form.
- **Not verified by native speakers.** The translations and pronunciation guides have **not** been reviewed by native speakers of any language other than English. Expect some mistakes, awkward word choices and translations that don't fit every context. Please check with a dictionary, a teacher or a native speaker before relying on them.
